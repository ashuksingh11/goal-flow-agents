DEVICE AGENT HARNESS — AS BUILT

Nine harnesses running on the Family Hub device agent, validated against the implementation
# Harness Purpose Role Family Hub Examples
1 Pre-check Engine Confirm the world is ready before anything is spent or done.
Probes runtime conditions before planning
Checks again at the moment of acting
Blocks with a fix, not an error code
Samsung account signed in
Internet and SmartThings reachable
Oven, dishwasher or fridge online
Payment set up before an order
2 Capability Manager Know what the product can do, and what the agent may touch.
Discovers every module and function
Separates reading from acting
Hides prohibited capabilities entirely
Food inventory and recipes
Calendar, reminders, family profiles
Shopping list and deliveries
Appliances, security, notifications
3 Task Manager Turn one goal into tracked work with a lifecycle.
Breaks a goal into tasks and dependencies
Tracks each one through to completion
Pauses, retries and cancels
Weekly meal planning
Home-away preparation
Birthday party and guest dinner
Grocery cost and energy saving
4 Context & Grounding Engine Read the real world before deciding anything.
Gathers live product and household state
Lets the model read, never write
Anchors every date to today
What is in the fridge, and what expires
Calendar and busy evenings
Household dietary rules and profiles
Recent activity, deliveries, security state
5 Goal Planner Compose a dated, explained plan from grounded facts.
Weighs options against household rules
Puts a date on every step
Records what it rejected, and why
A week of dinners, each with its reason
A two-phase away plan: before leaving, on return
A dish turned down on preference, not rule
6 Safety Policy Engine Decide what may happen at all, and what needs consent.
Grades every action A0, A1, A2 or AX
Enforces household rules on every call
Screens results before the model sees them
A0 — mark an item as used
A1 — add to the shopping list
A2 — place an order, hold a delivery
AX — prohibited, with no approval path
7 Approval Manager Nothing that touches the real world happens without a person.
Freezes acting calls into a proposal ledger
Presents them as one decision
Executes only what was approved
Approve the week's shopping list
Approve holding deliveries while away
Approve the away routine and arming security
Decline one item without losing the plan
8 Product API Adapter The single seam between the agent and the product.
Every product read and write passes through it
Absorbs differences between product APIs
Swap the product, keep the agent
Inventory and recipe services
Calendar and family profile services
Appliance and security control
Deliveries and notifications
9 Monitor & Adapt Keep the plan true after it has been approved.
Watches each goal's slice of the world
Judges whether a change actually matters
Re-plans only the part affected
Food arrives, or is about to expire
A guest list changes
Another goal makes some days unusable
A quiet day — nothing proposed
As built:  13 capability modules  ·  37 functions  ·  6 goal domains  ·  4 safety rule kinds
Where the build differs from the spec
The Goal Planner is real but has no module of its own — it lives in the agent core.
AX is implemented and enforced; no Family Hub capability is graded AX in the shipped policy today.
Product APIs sit behind the real seam, but are backed by mock documents rather than live services.
